Combo Offer: Combo Offer: Buy 'Chilli Guava Cooler' + 'Waffle' together for ₹249 (save ~15%).
- Terms: Both items must be purchased in a single order. No substitutions. Valid all week, dine-in and delivery.
- Target Audience: Customers who order beverages and are open to dessert add-ons.
- Timing: All day, but especially promoted during peak beverage hours (15:00–21:00).
- Success Metrics: Redemption rate, incremental basket size, combo-driven revenue, margin per combo.
Reasoning: This combo leverages the highest lift pair (Lift: 29.24) from the lift analysis, indicating customers who buy 'Chilli Guava Cooler' are 29x more likely to also buy 'Waffle'. Both items are popular and complementary (drink + dessert). The offer is attractive, encourages trial, and increases basket size, while the discount is moderate to protect margins.
Cost Analysis: Assumptions: 2% redemption rate (based on similar F&B offers), 5 orders/day, incremental basket uplift of ₹60/order, 15% discount per combo (₹44), 30-day campaign.
- Redemption: 150 combos/month.
- Incremental Revenue: ₹37,350 (150 x ₹249).
- Discount Cost: ₹6,600 (150 x ₹44).
- Net Incremental Profit: ₹30,750 (before food cost; assuming 60% gross margin, net profit ≈ ₹18,450).
- Break-even: ~45 combos/month.
- ROI Timeline: <1 month.
- Risks: Cannibalization of full-price sales (mitigated by targeting add-on behavior), operational complexity (minimal, as both items are standard menu).

Threshold Offer: Threshold Offer: Get ₹50 off on orders above ₹420.
- Terms: Minimum spend of ₹420 required. Discount auto-applied. Valid all week, all channels.
- Target Audience: Customers with average or above-average spend, especially groups.
- Timing: All day, with extra push during weekends and peak hours.
- Success Metrics: % of orders crossing threshold, AOV uplift, incremental revenue, discount cost.
Reasoning: The 80th percentile order value is ₹419.04, making ₹420 a psychologically effective and data-driven threshold. This nudges customers just above the current high-value order segment (20% of orders), encouraging upsell without excessive discounting. The ₹50 discount is meaningful but not margin-eroding.
Cost Analysis: Assumptions: 5% redemption rate among eligible orders, 570 redemptions/month (20% of 11,367 orders), 30% of redemptions are true incremental (rest would have qualified anyway), average uplift per incremental order: ₹60, total discount cost: ₹28,500 (570 x ₹50).
- Incremental Revenue: ₹10,260 (171 x ₹60).
- Net Incremental Profit: Discount cost offset by increased sales; with 60% margin, net profit ≈ ₹6,156.
- Break-even: ~475 redemptions/month.
- ROI Timeline: 1–2 months.
- Risks: Discount leakage to non-incremental orders (mitigated by threshold tuning), possible check-splitting (monitor and adjust).

Happy Hours Offer: Happy Hours Offer: 20% off on all orders placed Monday–Wednesday, 11:00–14:00.
- Terms: Valid for dine-in and delivery. No minimum spend. Excludes combos and threshold offers.
- Target Audience: Value-seeking customers, students, office-goers.
- Timing: Off-peak days and hours (Monday–Wednesday, 11:00–14:00) identified as slowest periods in temporal analysis.
- Success Metrics: Order volume uplift during offer window, revenue per hour, new vs repeat customer ratio.
Reasoning: Order analysis shows Monday–Wednesday and 11:00–14:00 are the lowest-traffic periods. A 20% discount is strong enough to shift demand without eroding peak period margins. Excluding other offers prevents stacking and protects profitability.
Cost Analysis: Assumptions: 10% uplift in orders during offer window (from 394 to 433 orders/month in the slot), average order value ₹200, 20% discount, 3 days/week, 4 weeks.
- Incremental Orders: 118/month.
- Incremental Revenue: ₹23,600 (118 x ₹200).
- Discount Cost: ₹8,660 (433 x ₹200 x 20%).
- Net Incremental Profit: With 60% margin, net profit ≈ ₹5,664.
- Break-even: ~90 incremental orders/month.
- ROI Timeline: 1–2 months.
- Risks: Discount abuse (limit to one per customer/day), operational strain (low risk due to off-peak timing).

Combined Cost Analysis: Portfolio Effect: Combo increases basket size, threshold boosts AOV, happy hours fills slow slots—together, they maximize revenue across all dayparts and customer segments. Offers are non-overlapping and mutually reinforcing.
- Total Investment: ~₹43,760/month in discounts.
- Total Incremental Revenue: ~₹71,210/month.
- Total Net Incremental Profit: ~₹30,270/month (after discounts, before food cost; with 60% margin, net profit ≈ ₹18,162/month).
- ROI Timeline: Full payback in 1–2 months.
- Implementation Priority: 1) Threshold Offer (broadest impact), 2) Combo Offer (high-margin upsell), 3) Happy Hours (targeted traffic boost). Launch in this order for maximum early ROI and learning.
- Success Benchmarks: 3%+ overall order uplift, 10% AOV increase, 15% off-peak order growth, positive net profit within 60 days.
- Risks: Offer cannibalization, discount stacking, operational errors. Mitigate via clear terms, staff training, and ongoing monitoring.
