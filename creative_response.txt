### 1. DATA ANALYSIS SUMMARY

- **Order Volume & Revenue:** The restaurant processed 11,367 orders, generating ₹33.8 lakh in revenue, with an average order value (AOV) of ₹297.79 and an average basket of just 1.37 unique items.
- **Order Patterns:**
  - **Peak Days:** Sunday is dominant (29.3% of all orders), followed by Saturday and Thursday.
  - **Peak Hours:** Business peaks from 3 pm to 9 pm, especially between 4–6 pm.
  - **Off-Peak:** Lunchtime (11 am–2 pm) is underutilized; late-night and very early morning are negligible.
- **Order Value Distribution:** 20% of orders are high value (above ₹419), but most orders are small—single-item baskets are overwhelmingly the norm.
- **Cross-Sell Potential:** Multiple high-lift item pairs (e.g., “Chilli Guava Cooler + Waffle” with a lift of 29.2) present clear bundled upsell opportunities, though actual pair frequency is still low.
- **Co-occurrence Hotspots:** Some item pairs are naturally bought together >50 times, but “most common basket size = 1” shows underutilized cross-sell.

---

### 2. STRATEGIC RATIONALE

- **Low basket size** means lost revenue; proven product affinities (strong lift/co-occurrence) allow us to confidently incentivize combos.
- **Afternoon lull** (pre-peak) and weekdays (Mon/Tues/Wed) are prime for footfall stimulation—most sales concentrate into a few peak periods.
- **High-value segments** (top 20% spenders/orders) should be targeted for loyalty or frequency enhancement—retaining them is critical.
- Platforms are squeezing margins; incentivized in-store visits/call-and-pickup must outcompete delivery platforms in perceived value.

---

### 3. COUPON RECOMMENDATIONS

#### 1. "Afternoon Power Combo" Coupon
- **Coupon Description:** “Order any Shake + Waffle Combo between 12–3 pm and get 20% OFF (max discount ₹60)”
- **Target Segment:** All dine-in customers, focused on single-item basket buyers; tap strong shake–waffle pairing (esp. “Chilli Guava Cooler” + “Waffle”)
- **Timing Strategy:** Mon–Fri, 12–3 pm (off-peak, pre-peak hours)
- **Expected Impact:** 10–15% footfall increase in targeted hours; projected increase of AOV by 25–30% in those periods.

#### 2. "Bundled Bliss" Upsize Coupon
- **Coupon Description:** “Buy Any Signature Shake, Add a Second Signature Shake for 50% OFF”
- **Target Segment:** Existing customers frequently ordering only one shake (most common pattern), especially during weekends.
- **Timing Strategy:** Sat–Sun, 3–6 pm (bridge late lunch to peak dinner rush, monetize waiting time)
- **Expected Impact:** At least 7–9% uplift in multi-shake orders, creates habit for higher basket sizes.

#### 3. "High-Spender Loyalty Bounceback"
- **Coupon Description:** “Spent ₹450+ in one visit? Next visit within 10 days: Enjoy a 25% OFF on any bill above ₹299 (max ₹100 discount)”
- **Target Segment:** Top 20% of customers (AOV >₹419)—frequency boosters to encourage repeat visits and retention.
- **Timing Strategy:** Dynamic—personalized voucher issued post high-value transaction (valid any day, to counteract platform churn).
- **Expected Impact:** 5–8% conversion on issued coupons; increased frequency from high-value segment worth significant incremental revenue.

#### 4. "Lunch Hour Saver"
- **Coupon Description:** “Weekday Lunch: Any Shake + Any Main @ ₹299 flat (vs avg. normal ₹370+).”
- **Target Segment:** Office crowd, students, and value-seeking weekday traffic.
- **Timing Strategy:** Mon–Fri, 12–2 pm
- **Expected Impact:** Up to 20% rise in lunchtime orders; effective defense against platform-discounted delivery slotting.

---

### 4. IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE

- **A/B Test:** Pilot each coupon for 2–3 weeks; compare order volume, AOV, and daypart revenues with matched historical periods (store vs. platform sales).
- **Metrics to Track:** Redemptions, incremental revenue (net of discounts), basket size changes, footfall by hour/day, repeat visit rate for high-value segment.
- **Mitigation:** Cap discounts per bill/customer per day. Monitor for cannibalization (baseline—ensure customers aren’t shifting existing spend to discounted slots).

**Example: Afternoon Power Combo**
- Avg off-peak order value: ~₹230

- **Break-even:** Achieved if combo boosts AOV by at least 15% and increases item count per order by 0.5+.
